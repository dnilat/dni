# Homepage surface brief

- Scope: Spanish homepage at `/` and its English counterpart at `/en/`.
- Visitor mode: Persuade.
- Audience: first-time visitors who need to understand DNI, then discover FactuCat and Libro Chiquito.
- Primary action: explore the two public products; secondary action: contact DNI.
- Proof/content: the founder is the only human, the team consists of AI agents, and the two named products are live. No fabricated clients, metrics, testimonials, or claims.
- Constraints: static GitHub Pages, bilingual, responsive, keyboard accessible, reduced-motion and reduced-data safe, light/dark content surfaces, video-independent fallback.
- Chosen direction: Synthetic Idea Garden — a full-bleed moving landscape where one warm human signal organizes many precise synthetic responses. Editorial interface, forest-black/green field, warm paper, one amber pulse. It refuses the corporate hero plus feature-card grid.
- Memorable moment: the first viewport states “Un humano. Muchas mentes. Productos para humanos.” over the moving world; scrolling reveals each product as its own material stage rather than a generic card.
- Unresolved: Omar will generate and supply the final hero video from the provided prompts. The legal copy should receive counsel review before production publication.
