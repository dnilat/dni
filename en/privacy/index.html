What this notice covers

This notice applies only to the dni.lat corporate website operated by Donde Nacen las Ideas SA de CV ("DNI"). FactuCat and Libro Chiquito have their own services, data flows, and privacy notices on their respective websites.

Information we process

When you email us

The website opens your email application through a mailto: link. If you choose to write, we receive the information in your message, usually your name, email address, and the contents of the conversation.

Technical delivery data

The website is served through web-hosting infrastructure. The provider may process technical data such as IP address, browser, date, time, and requested resources to deliver the site, prevent abuse, and maintain security. DNI does not add analytics tools, advertising pixels, or behavioral profiles to this website.

Theme preference

We store your light or dark theme preference in your browser under the key dni-theme. It stays on your device and is not used to identify or track you.

How we use information

  • To reply to messages, questions, or proposals you send us.
  • To maintain the website's availability, integrity, and security.
  • To comply with legal obligations or valid requests from authorities.

We do not use information from this website for behavioral advertising, commercial profiling, or selling personal data.

Processors, disclosures, and external links

Hosting, email, and security providers may process information on DNI's behalf to provide their services. We may also disclose information when required by law or a competent authority. We do not sell or rent personal data.

Links to FactuCat, Libro Chiquito, and other external websites open independent services governed by their own privacy notices and terms.

Retention and security

We retain email conversations only as long as needed to reply, follow the relationship, or comply with legal obligations. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no transmission or storage system is infallible.

Your privacy rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, or objection to the processing of your personal data and withdraw consent where applicable. Email hola@dni.lat with your name, a clear description of your request, and a way to receive our response. We may request reasonable information to verify your identity.

Changes to this policy

We may update this notice when the website, our providers, or applicable obligations change. The current version will always show its update date on this page.
