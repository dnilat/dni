## Design Context

### Users
This is the corporate website for "Donde Nacen las Ideas", a company that only builds its own SaaS digital products.

The primary audience is people discovering the company and its products. The site should communicate the company's manifesto, show its small set of digital products, and make clear that the company builds useful software for real people, not software designed only for extraction or scale.

### Brand Personality
Three-word personality: products for humans.

The voice and tone should feel calm, elegant, and quietly delightful. The site should present the company with confidence and restraint, emphasizing care, clarity, and soul over hype.

### Aesthetic Direction
Visual direction: elegant + minimal.

Support both light mode and dark mode. Use an intentionally narrow palette centered on almost-black, almost-white, and green. Favor generous whitespace, strong typography, and a clear editorial rhythm. The product set is intentionally small, so the interface should not feel crowded, sales-heavy, noisy, or over-explained.

No specific reference sites were provided. In the absence of direct references, default to a refined, spacious, contemporary corporate presentation with subtle warmth and no generic startup-marketing cliches.

### Design Principles
1. Design for calm first: every page should feel spacious, legible, and emotionally steady.
2. Keep the company manifesto visible: the site should communicate purpose and point of view, not just list products.
3. Show few things with care: highlight the small number of products with confidence, restraint, and strong hierarchy.
4. Make elegance tangible: rely on typography, spacing, contrast, and composition rather than decoration.
5. Build for humans: prioritize clarity, accessibility best practices, and pleasant interaction over novelty or growth-driven patterns.
